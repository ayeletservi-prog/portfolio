About Me

Designing with curiosity,
delivering with intention.

I’m a Senior Product Designer who specializes in making complex systems clear and intuitive, with 20 years of experience designing enterprise products and data-rich applications.

My background in Cognitive Psychology shapes the way I approach design — seeking to understand user behavior, decision-making, and the context in which people interact with products. I believe great UX starts with deep understanding before moving into solutions.

I lead end-to-end design, from research and workflow analysis to user flows, interaction design, and detailed specifications. I collaborate closely with product managers and engineers to create experiences that balance user needs, business goals, and technical constraints.

Throughout my career, I’ve designed complex systems for professional users across defense, operational, and enterprise domains. These experiences have taught me that successful UX is not just about simplifying interfaces — it’s about understanding the complexity behind them and creating solutions that help people work more effectively.
