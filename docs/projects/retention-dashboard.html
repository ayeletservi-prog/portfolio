Dashboard Verint Data Visualization

Retention Dashboard

Designed a dashboard that provides a clear view of retention status and trends across organizations and media types.

Overview

The design aims to give tenants a self-service window into their interaction storage — visibility into how it's being archived and deleted, and forecasting to plan future needs — without requiring Verint involvement. The dashboard organizes storage information into layers, from a high-level health check down to granular detail by data type and line of business, letting administrators monitor trends and understand usage on their own.

The Problem

Tenants had no visibility into how their recorded interactions were stored, archived, or deleted. They couldn't tell whether archiving was working correctly, whether expired calls were being purged as expected, or how much storage they would need in the future.

The goal was to give tenants a self-service window into their storage — to monitor its health, understand usage trends, and forecast future needs, all without Verint involvement.

01

Understanding the Needs

Two core user needs shaped the design direction:

  • Visibility — tenants need to see the current state of their storage: what is being archived, what is expiring, and whether deletions are happening as configured.
  • Forecasting — based on historical data, tenants want to predict future storage requirements so they can plan ahead and avoid surprises.

A third need, control — the ability to extend or reduce archival duration per line of business, without depending on Verint services — was identified but fell outside the scope of this phase.

02

Ideation & Design

The design organizes storage information into layers: an overall, tenant-wide summary at the top, with the option to drill down per organization or per data type. At every level, administrators can see historical usage data alongside a trend line.

03

Outcome & Impact

As of July 2026, this project has not been implemented. Once live, the dashboard is expected to give tenants something they've never had before: a complete, self-service view of their storage health. Administrators would be able to verify that archiving and deletion are working as expected, and anticipate capacity needs from trend data — all without submitting a support request to Verint.
