Capture Rules

Giving tenant administrators direct, safe control over interaction capture — without relying on Verint services.

Overview

This project gave tenant administrators direct, safe control over interaction capture — designed first and foremost for a new, customer-based Business Rule Administrator role, with view-only or view-plus-limited-edit access for other business users, alongside IT administrators — replacing a support-ticket-driven workflow with a self-service experience. The solution paired a guided configuration wizard with an at-a-glance view of existing rules, eliminating the need for Verint services involvement in routine capture changes.

The Problem

Every time a customer needed to adjust which interactions were recorded, they had to submit a request to Verint — creating delays, operational costs, and an ongoing dependency on internal support.

The business goal was clear: reduce the cost and friction of managing capture rules by giving tenant administrators direct, safe control — without requiring Verint involvement for routine changes.

01

Understanding the Needs

Working from a detailed requirements spec and clarifying questions with the PM, I mapped the two distinct needs that the product had to serve: configuring new rules, and quickly understanding existing rules, including what each one does, its conditions, and its current status.

I identified four core requirements:

  • Selectively capture interactions. Not every call needs to be recorded; users want to capture only what meets specific conditions.
  • Block specific interactions. Administrators need to prevent recording of certain content types or channels based on compliance or business requirements.
  • Understand existing rules. Administrators need a quick, clear view of what rules are currently active for a given org, employee group, or individual employee.
  • Monitor rule performance. Users need to verify rules are triggering as intended, including how many times a rule fired in the last 24 hours and when it last triggered.
The design challenge

With no existing user interface to build on, the challenge was to transform a complex technical capability into a clear, end-to-end experience — enabling users to create capture rules and monitor their performance over time.

02

Ideation & Design

I designed two primary experiences to serve the users. The first — rule visibility — gave administrators an instant read on what rules are active, their scope, and their trigger statistics, without needing to enter edit mode. The second — rule configuration — guided users through creation via a step-by-step wizard that abstracted away the complexity of the underlying Business Rules engine, with access scoped to each role's permissions.

03

Outcome & Impact

The solution removed the need for Verint services involvement in routine rule changes, reducing support costs and giving customers genuine operational independence. Business Rule Administrators, other business users, and IT administrators could now self-serve their most common capture management tasks directly through the interface.
